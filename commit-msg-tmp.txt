docs: fix a fabricated causal claim and four incorporation defects

An independent fidelity review of the v3.4 incorporation found a claim I
invented and several real contradictions.

The worst was a fabricated causal link. Both the build plan and
AGENTS.md asserted that guessing at a catalog's facet vocabulary "is how
the topic-dictionary defect happened." Those are two different defects
in two different systems: the topic-dictionary defect was an Open
Library query shipped without a juvenile filter, caught by the coverage
matrix, and has nothing to do with facets being absent from a catalog
feed. Merging them into one story would have misled anyone trying to
prevent a recurrence. Both are now stated separately, with the explicit
note that neither is evidence for the other.

Records the library-catalog-as-corpus decision the owner approved,
instead of importing catalog-shaped work silently. Section 2.3 currently
scopes the catalog to availability only, so this checkpoint is blocked
until the owner amends that invariant rather than merely approving the
checkpoint. Open Library remains the verified metadata provider, and the
candidate pool and provenance model are provider-neutral and carry over.

Checkpoint 7D excluded a user-facing bag while requiring twenty real
logged outcomes, which is impossible without one, and shipped the
highest-frequency control in the product with no design gate. Its
relationship to Checkpoint 8 is now explicit, and the one-tap control
requires an interactive review with three or more options.

Restores the dismiss-state table, without which child_love — which the
delight-rate formula depends on — was undefined anywhere; the bootstrap
confidence bands, seeding guidance, bounded-exploration rule, and the
requirement never to average child and caregiver reactions; and the
rejected-approaches register, whose reopen-if conditions are what stop
settled questions being relitigated.

Reconciles two contradictions: G3 can recommend dropping the
deterministic engine long before Checkpoint 11A authorizes it, so a G3
result is now evidence carried forward and authorizes evaluation only;
and delight rate is named authoritative for the north-star question
while the existing success metrics continue to govern product health.
