docs: close the discovery-drift hole and three evaluation defects

An independent recommender-systems review found a structural flaw in the
gate design plus several evaluation problems that would have produced
wrong decisions.

The serious one: nothing re-checked discovery after G5 passed. G6 scored
only aggregate delight rate, which structurally rewards conservatism,
since a book similar to a known love is a near-certain hit while a
genuine discovery pick is high variance. Optimization could therefore
have tuned the engine back toward safe canon-adjacent picks, undoing the
north star while every gate still read PASS. G6 now requires a
new-creator delight-rate trend as a second condition, and a flat or
declining trend routes to re-diagnosis even when the aggregate looks
healthy.

G3's ground truth is the household's own historically found books, which
the north star describes as canon-leaning, so recall against it rewards
reproducing the canon. G3 is now scoped to detecting decisively worse or
suspiciously better rather than ranking close methods, owner judgment is
blind and pairwise because articulate model explanations persuade
independently of pick quality, an explicit-negative set is required so
precision can be checked at all, and the trivial baseline is defined as
most-held-in-age-band rather than a strawman.

G4 fired at roughly the sample size where the confidence ramp has barely
engaged personalization, while the new-creator quota forces high-variance
picks at the same moment, so it now allows for both and is scored on the
non-exploration portion.

Delight rate gains a maturity window, since a reread cannot occur in the
first days a book is home and counting fresh borrows would manufacture a
pivot out of arithmetic.

Also: holdings are described as less confounded by popularity rather than
independent of it, band position is normalized by years since
publication because a new book and a fading one occupy the same band for
opposite reasons, summary embeddings are computed during the corpus pass
where they are nearly free rather than forcing a second enumeration, and
heading weights are damped like personalization is.
