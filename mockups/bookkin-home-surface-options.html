What is being decided

Two things independently. Visual direction switches between Refined Brighter and Original Bright Snap, which stays an open owner decision until Checkpoint 7P. Layout switches the three home-surface proposals.

Each layout has a different subject: a spread of reasons, a trip, a book. Use Day one to see each with no household history.

1 · The Mix

Every book is here for a visibly different kind of reason, and the variety of reasons is the structure.

for Keeps creators you already like as a first-class reason, sitting beside "someone new to you" rather than being crowded out by it.

for Unlike the rejected Bag, every book carries a checkable warrant, and the warrants differ in kind.

for The sticker colors make composition visible. If new-creator picks quietly drained away over time, the page would go monochrome.

against Five books is more to take in than three, and the reason types must stay genuinely distinct or the stickers become decoration.

day one Shows the three reason types it can fill honestly and drops the two it cannot.

2 · The Trip

The unit is a library trip, not a recommendation.

for The only direction present at the moment delight rate's denominator is set — when the caregiver decides what to physically carry home.

for Last trip's books sit at the top. At one trip a fortnight, reliably closing out the previous trip may be worth more than any ranking change.

for "Add a book I picked up" captures unprompted shelf finds — a signal no data source carries, and the one the plan now requires to be recorded rather than inferred.

for The list is stable between visits, which fixes the staleness problem the rejected Bag could not answer.

against Risks drifting into logistics software, where quality stops being the subject.

against Close-out may never happen; it has to be inferred from the next open.

Note: the two ticked boxes represent caregiver action already taken, not a preselected default. No reaction is ever preselected.

3 · Because You Loved

Anchored on a book that actually worked, with recommendations hanging off it.

for Leads with the strongest signal available — a comp title from the household's own history beats any topic, and converts an unknown book into a known quantity.

day one Weakest, and deliberately shown refusing to fill itself with popular titles it cannot attribute to this child.

against Narrow if the anchor is stale or taste has moved.

Corrections after design audit

The previous revision of this file had real defects, now fixed:

  • It showed only one visual direction. Section 3 requires both tracks in parallel until 7P. The toggle is restored, matching Checkpoint 7A.
  • It regressed to beige. Canvas had moved to #fdf7ec with brown lines and text — the exact regression section 3 and docs/design/principles.md forbid. Tokens now match 7A: canvas #f4f7f8, ink #111316, yellow #ffe500.
  • Cyan had been deleted and replaced with two invented hues that were never used. The joy now comes from the approved set: yellow, cyan, rose, blue, ink.
  • A reaction was preselected — "Loved it" shipped pressed, violating the invariant that reactions are never preselected.
  • Reaction controls were 38px, below the 44px floor and the 48px preference for reaction controls.
  • Two contrast failures on the joy elements: coral eyebrow at 2.53:1 and white-on-mint at 2.46:1.
  • Copy narrated the system's own reasoning and used internal vocabulary. Rationale moved here, where it belongs.
  • Focus-visible, reduced-motion, tablet width, and a live region were all missing.

One deliberate disagreement

"Popular" was suggested for the library-selection signal. I would avoid that specific word: holdings measure professional selection under budget, and the engine deliberately targets the widely-chosen-but-not-famous band. Calling that "popular" would describe the opposite of what was done.

Award and selection claims have been removed from the cards entirely for now, rather than shown as unverified placeholders. They return once there is a verified source to cite.

Not represented here

  • The aisle view behind "I'm at the library".
  • Single-candidate results, which are the expected case when retrieval is working.
  • Multi-child and second-caregiver states.
