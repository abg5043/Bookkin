What is being decided

All three open on the recommendation. None opens on the shelf. Bright Snap is unchanged — this is information architecture only.

Each has a different subject: a spread of reasons, a trip, a book. Use Day one to see each with no household evidence, which is where they differ most.

1 · The Mix (replaces Someone New)

Every book is here for a visibly different kind of reason, and the variety of reasons is the structure. Five stickers, five colours, no two alike.

for Fixes the complaint that a single-creator page cuts off authors you already like. "More Julia Donaldson" is a first-class reason here, sitting next to "someone new to you" rather than being crowded out by it.

for Not the old Bag: every book carries a legible, checkable warrant, and the warrants are of different types. The old Bag showed three books with one kind of reason and no account of itself.

for The sticker colours make the composition rules visible at a glance. If new-creator picks quietly vanished over time, you would see it — the page would go monochrome.

against Five books is more to take in than three, and the reason types have to stay genuinely distinct or the stickers become decoration.

day one Honest and still useful: it shows the three reason types it can fill and says plainly which two it can't, rather than faking them.

2 · The Trip

The unit is a library trip, not a recommendation. Opens at the kitchen table, travels into the aisle, closes out at bedtime.

for The only direction present at the moment delight rate's denominator is actually set — when you decide what to physically carry home.

for Last trip's books sit at the top asking how they went. At one trip a fortnight, a page that reliably closes out the previous trip may be worth more than any ranking change.

for Added this round: "Add a book I picked up" captures the ones you find yourself — genuinely valuable evidence that no data source carries — and "More recommendations" extends the list without discarding it.

for The list is stable. "Same list as Tuesday" is a trust-building sentence, and it fixes the staleness problem the old Bag couldn't answer.

against Risks drifting into logistics software — a packing list with covers — where quality quietly stops being the subject.

against Close-out may never happen; it has to be inferred from the next open, not depend on a button you remember to press.

3 · Because You Loved (kept)

Anchored on a book that actually worked, with recommendations hanging off it and the reasoning as the organizing structure.

for Leads with the strongest signal available — a comp title from your own house beats any topic. It converts an unknown book into a known quantity, which is the core trust problem.

for Each pick now carries its independent judgement underneath the similarity reason. Similarity says like this; the second line says and good.

day one Weakest, and deliberately shown refusing to fake it. It needs one genuinely loved book and won't fill itself with popular titles.

against Narrow if the anchor is stale or taste has moved. One anchor per page.

Changed this round

  • "Held by 19 of 29 systems" is gone. It exposed the mechanism. Replaced with the human fact underneath it — librarians across the area chose it widely. Not "popular": see the note below.
  • Joy restored. Coloured covers, sticker badges with hard shadows, warmer paper, a reread star. The palette and type are still Bright Snap.
  • Known-liked creators are a first-class reason in all three, not a leak. The plan caps creator adjacency at one seat per bag; it never meant excluding them.

One deliberate disagreement

You suggested "Popular". I'd avoid that specific word, because it is the one thing this signal is not, and saying it would undercut the product.

Holdings measure how many librarians spent budget on a book — professional selection, not sales. The engine deliberately targets the widely-chosen-but-not-famous band and drops the top of it. Labelling that "popular" would describe the opposite of what was done, and it's the one claim a caregiver could catch out.

"Librarians across the area chose it" is the same fact in human terms, hides the mechanism just as well, and is the actual differentiator against a bestseller list.
