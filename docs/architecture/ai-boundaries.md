# AI boundaries

AI is optional assistance, never authority.

V0.1 requires deterministic candidate eligibility, scoring, composition, taste evidence, and recommendation explanations. No AI dependency, model, secret, or runtime is required for V0.1 completion.

A future local LLM may be evaluated only after deterministic quality passes and a separate human gate approves privacy, cost, latency, operations, and failure behavior. It remains behind `AIProvider` and may only reword approved explanation clauses from verified structured facts. A hosted LLM would be a separate scope expansion requiring its own provider and child-privacy decision gate.

AI must not:

- Source or invent candidate works.
- Create ISBNs, authors, metadata, history, interests, reactions, or preference evidence.
- Determine eligibility, score, rank, composition, or suppression.
- Claim library availability, holds, loans, or capabilities.
- Change a deterministic fact or hide material uncertainty.
- Block bag generation when unavailable.

Any AI output is schema-validated. Deterministic wording remains available as fallback and comparison. Provider responses and prompts do not contain unnecessary household or child data.
